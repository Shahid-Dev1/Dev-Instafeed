# Merchant guide: getting started and troubleshooting

## Getting started (about 10 minutes)
1. **Install** Instafeed from the Shopify App Store and open it. Your products sync automatically.
2. **Add videos** (Videos → Add videos):
   - upload files you own,
   - paste YouTube, Shorts or TikTok links,
   - or connect your TikTok or Instagram account if your plan includes it.
3. **Tag products** on each video (open the video → Tagged products), optionally choosing a variant.
4. **Create a widget** (Widgets → Create): pick Stories, Carousel, Floating, Banner, Grid or Product gallery. Add videos, style it, check the desktop and mobile preview, then **Publish**.
5. **Add it to your theme:**
   - **Floating videos:** Home → "Open theme editor", then turn on **Instafeed videos** under App embeds and Save.
   - **Other widgets:** in the theme editor, add the **Shoppable videos** block to a section and paste the widget ID (Widget → Copy).
6. **Track results** in Analytics: views, clicks, Add to Carts and attributed revenue.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Widget doesn't appear | Is it published? Is the block or app embed added and saved in the theme? Does the widget ID match? Changes can take up to 1 minute. Check Plan for a usage pause. |
| "Upgrade to unlock" | That feature or limit isn't in your plan. See Plan to compare and change. |
| Video stuck "processing" | Large uploads can take a few minutes. If it fails, upload an MP4 (H.264). |
| TikTok/Instagram "Reconnect" | Your authorization expired or was revoked. Reconnect from Videos → Add videos. |
| Instagram Reel "not importable" | Instagram doesn't provide files for Reels with licensed music. Use another Reel. |
| Products missing | Products → Sync products. Draft or archived products can be filtered by status. |
| No attributed revenue | Orders are attributed when a shopper adds a product from a video and buys within your attribution window, with analytics consent given in your cookie banner. |
| Analytics tool not receiving events | Integrations → Send test event. The tool's tag must already be installed on your store. |

Contact support from the app listing's support link. Include your store domain.
