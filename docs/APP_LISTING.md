# App Store listing draft

**Name:** Instafeed: Shoppable Videos _(confirm the name is available; avoid keyword stuffing)_

**Tagline (≤ 62 chars):** Turn videos into sales with shoppable stories and carousels

**Introduction:** Show your products in action. Add shoppable videos from your uploads, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, tag products, and let shoppers add to cart without leaving the video.

**Key benefits:**
- Six widget styles (Stories, Carousel, Floating, Banner, Grid, Product gallery) that match your theme, with desktop and mobile previews.
- Shoppable player with variant selection and Add to Cart, or go straight to the product page.
- Analytics and revenue attribution: views, clicks, Add to Carts and the orders your videos drive.
- Lightweight and accessible: under 10 KB of script, lazy loading, keyboard and reduced-motion support.
- Built for Shopify: theme app extension, no theme code edits.

**Pricing:** Free / Starter $19 / Growth $49 / Pro $149 per month (must match the App Pricing configuration).

**Assets still needed:** app icon (1200×1200), 3–6 screenshots (1600×900: widget builder, storefront carousel, player with product popup, analytics), optional demo video, demo store URL with a published widget, a hosted privacy policy URL, and a support email or URL.
