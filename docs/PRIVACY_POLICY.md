**What we collect from merchants:** store domain, name, currency and timezone; staff names and emails (team members); product catalog data (titles, variants, prices, images); videos you upload or import, and their metadata; app settings; plan and usage data; audit logs of actions in the app.

**What we collect about shoppers:** a random visitor identifier (no name, email or address) and interaction events with our widgets (views, clicks, add-to-cart), only where the store's cookie consent allows analytics. For orders: the order id, totals, line items and the visitor identifier attribute. We do **not** store customer names, emails, addresses or payment data.

**Why:** to provide the app (videos, widgets, Add to Cart), show analytics and revenue attribution, bill through Shopify, provide support, and keep the service secure.

**Processors:** [hosting provider], [database/Redis provider], Bunny.net (video hosting), Google (YouTube Data API), TikTok and Meta (only if you connect those accounts), Anthropic (only if you use the AI assistant; your instruction and widget settings are sent), and the analytics tools you configure.

**Retention:** while installed. On uninstall, access tokens are deleted immediately. All store data is deleted when Shopify sends the shop redaction request (48 hours after uninstall). Backups roll off within [14–35] days.

**Your rights:** access, correction and deletion requests to [email]. Shopify customer data requests are handled automatically via Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks.

**Security:** encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for credentials (AES-256-GCM), strict per-store isolation, least-privilege access, audited support access.
