# Pilot plan (10–20 D2C brands, ~6 weeks)

**Entry criteria:** staging sign-off; production deployed with backups and monitoring; App Pricing plans configured; privacy policy and terms published; support inbox staffed.

| Week | Activity | Success metric |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Recruit 10–20 brands (mix of beauty, fashion, food), unlisted install link, Pro plan comped via App Pricing discount | 10+ installs |
| 1 | Guided onboarding call (30 min); publish ≥1 widget per store | 80% publish within 7 days |
| 2–4 | Weekly check-ins; monitor errors, queue health and storefront performance | <1% 5xx; no tenant/security incidents; storefront script ≤ 12 KB |
| 4 | Review analytics with each brand | video CTR and Add-to-Cart rate baselines; ≥ 50% of brands see attributed orders |
| 5–6 | Fix the top issues; collect testimonials and reviews | NPS ≥ 30; 3+ case studies |

**Exit (go to public listing):** the success metrics above are met, no open P0/P1 bugs, the App Store review checklist passes, and the provider approvals are known (TikTok/Instagram either approved or kept behind flags).

**Rollback for a pilot store:** disable the app embed or blocks (instant), or uninstall. Data is deleted per DATA_DELETION.md.
